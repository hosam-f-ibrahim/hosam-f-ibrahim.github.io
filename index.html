How I can help

In a full-time role, or on a contract or project basis.

Financial intelligence systems

Architecture and technical design for systems that cover a financial intelligence unit's operating cycle, from registration, report collection and validation, through screening, enrichment, analysis and case management, to intelligence disseminated to law enforcement; and reporting-side tools that help compliance officers submit compliant reports.

Where I've done it

I led the architecture, design and delivery of FIU360, a modular platform that runs the full operating cycle of national financial intelligence units to FATF and UN standards: registration with role-based access; report collection through a configurable XML engine (300+ parameters; CTR, STR, EFT, AIF); sanctions screening; enrichment from financial, national, international and open sources; AI-driven search and LLM-powered operational and strategic analytics; interactive relationship graphs; case and document management; one-click FATF statistics; and automated dissemination to law enforcement. Report intake alone shows the depth of the engineering: every submitted report is validated against its XML schema and a business-rule engine, and every object it carries, from reports, persons, entities, accounts, transactions and identifications to many more, is extracted with its relationships and integrated as linked, investigation-ready intelligence. The intake pipeline sustains 100K+ records an hour end to end, measured on a commodity server. On the reporting side of the exchange, I delivered AML PRO, which compliance officers at reporting entities use to submit compliant XML reports from day one.

System and data integration

Connecting systems that were not built to work together: ETL pipelines, enrichment from financial, government and open sources, cross-database connectors, REST and web-service integration, and third-party API providers, with validation, error handling and monitoring built in.

Where I've done it

I architected and built FIU360 Connect, the enrichment engine that operates on top of existing systems: the moment a report arrives, or whenever an analyst asks, it reaches out to financial, national, international and open sources, then cleanses, deduplicates and normalizes what comes back through parallel, multithreaded ETL pipelines, so analysts receive investigation-ready data instead of gathering it by hand. It delivers 99% data accuracy, cut acquisition latency by 99%, and reduced enrichment failures by more than 90% through validation, error handling and retry logic, monitored in production with Application Insights. The same discipline runs through the enhanced visualization add-on's dynamic connectors, which map relationships across external databases into one unified view, with REST services for real-time data; AML PRO's data managers, which bring reporting entities' data in from spreadsheets and organizational databases and correct inconsistencies before submission; and, earlier in my career, real-time OPC data streams from industrial automation servers and finance, HR and payroll systems integrated with the enterprise applications around them.

Legacy migration and extending existing systems

Migrating data and functionality from legacy systems to new platforms, and closing business gaps with add-ons on top of systems already in use, with attention to data integrity and minimal disruption.

Where I've done it

At DPME, I led the team that designed and built an ERP covering project management, finance, HR, payroll and timesheets, automated the workflows that cut administrative overhead by more than 50%, and migrated the legacy data into it with integrity intact and minimal disruption to day-to-day operations. The other half of this work is knowing when not to replace a system that still carries the business, and extending it instead: FIU360 Connect and the enhanced visualization add-on extend FIU360 and other existing FIU systems without changing their code base, and at intelliSYS I designed add-on and plugin extensions that closed business gaps in existing systems, along with data-transformation solutions that carried legacy systems onto new platforms.
