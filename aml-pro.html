AML PRO

Jan 2022 – Dec 2022 · intelliSYS Solutions & Consulting

The reporting side of the exchange: an application that compliance officers at reporting entities use to prepare and submit compliant XML reports to their financial intelligence unit from day one. I delivered it.

At a glance

My role
Delivered AML PRO; implemented the .NET/C# application for FIU XML reporting, including client-data import, validation and transformation
Built for
Compliance officers at banks and other reporting entities
Output
Compliant XML reports for submission to the FIU, and PDF case reports
Stack
C#/.NET; Microsoft SQL Server; XML Schema; DevExpress; Git

The problem

Every bank and other reporting entity has to file reports with its financial intelligence unit in the strict XML format the unit defines. The data that goes into those reports lives in spreadsheets and in the entity's own databases, in its own shapes, and much of it is incomplete or inconsistent. A report that fails the unit's validation is rejected, and every rejection costs the compliance team time.

Clean data before it becomes a report

AML PRO puts the work where the problems start, and I built the two data managers at its heart. An External Data Manager imports client data from standard Excel files or directly from organizational databases, and transformation mappings cleanse, deduplicate and normalize it into one standard form, however different the sources are.

A Client Data Manager then validates every client record against the reporting requirements: it checks completeness, flags inconsistencies with suggested corrections, and lets officers fill gaps, attach documents and record why each change was made, so the audit trail stays intact. A verified client profile is reused across every report that needs it, instead of being corrected again each time.

From clean data to compliant reports

Around that data, AML PRO screens client names against international sanctions lists with configurable matching sensitivity, builds client and transaction profiles to surface unusual activity, visualizes flows of money and links between people and legal entities, and manages cases from confirmation to escalation. A case closes into a formal report in XML for the unit or in PDF, and AML PRO tracks each submission through to the unit's receipt confirmation. Every import, screening, profiling and case action is logged for audit.

How I worked

I worked with compliance, legal and business stakeholders to capture the requirements and keep the application aligned with what financial intelligence units mandate. I built the UI for compliance officers with DevExpress controls for fast editing and annotation, managed the code with pull-request reviews and branching strategies, and mentored junior engineers on ETL design, data validation and .NET development.

The same years gave me the other side of the exchange: leading FIU360's architecture, I knew what a unit does with each report after it arrives, and AML PRO was built to send it what the unit can use.
